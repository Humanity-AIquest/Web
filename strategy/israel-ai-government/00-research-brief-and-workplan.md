## 1. What the research changed about the task

Five findings change how the strategy has to be written:

1. **The election is on 27 October 2026, five weeks from now.** The 25th Knesset served its
   full term. After that come the count, the President's consultations, and coalition
   talks (28 days plus a possible 14-day extension under Basic Law: The Government).
   Realistically the "next government" is sworn in between late December 2026 and
   February 2027. Its first big test is the 2027 State Budget and the Arrangements Law
   (חוק ההסדרים). **That budget/Arrangements Law window is where our strategy can
   actually become policy.**
   - [Times of Israel: elections set for 27 Oct 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/judge-sets-date-for-next-scheduled-elections-for-october-2026/)
   - [Al Jazeera: Knesset confirms 27 October](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/12/israel-to-hold-national-elections-on-october-27-parliament-says)
   - [Chatham House: "Israel's general election will be like no other"](https://www.chathamhouse.org/2026/07/israels-general-election-will-be-no-other)
   - [IFES Election Guide](https://www.electionguide.org/elections/id/5092/) · [Wikipedia overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)

2. **Israel already has a national AI machine. We are not starting from zero.** In October
   2025 a National AI Directorate was set up inside the Prime Minister's Office, headed by
   Brig.-Gen. (res.) Erez Askal. A preliminary plan was approved on 17 May 2026, and the full
   National AI Program on 16 June 2026. Its targets are 100,000 GPUs for government and
   national use, 5,000 frontier GPUs a year from 2027 to 2032, a quantum computer tender
   ("Project Nexus"), and AI education. Critics describe it as the *fourth* national AI plan
   in seven years. They also point to no committed compute budget and a freeze on
   grid connections for data centres. **So our pitch is to complete and legitimise the
   existing program, not to replace it.** It adds what the program is missing: public trust,
   an open-source community, and a constitutional safety layer.
   - [PM Office: Government approves National AI Program (16 Jun 2026)](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/israel/2026/06/israel-260616-israel-pm01.htm)
   - [PM Netanyahu at launch of the program (10 Aug 2026)](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/israel/2026/08/israel-260810-israel-pm01.htm)
   - [JPost: Askal appointed to lead AI Directorate](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-870258)
   - [Ctech: PMO takes direct control of AI policy](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/ab6v2b1ra) (NIS 120M budget, 20 posts)
   - [JPost: "Israel's AI strategy remains fragmented"](http://www.jpost.com/business-and-innovation/tech-and-start-ups/article-905968)
   - [JNS: national AI plan approved](https://www.jns.org/news/israel-news/israel-approves-national-ai-plan-to-become-technology-powerhouse)
   - [Israel Tech Insider: "National AI Program Stalls"](https://www.israeltechinsider.com/p/national-ai-program-stalls-stealthy)
   - [Innovation Authority: National Program for AI (PDF, May 2025)](https://innovationisrael.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/AI-National-Program-en-14.5.25.pdf) · [aiisrael.org.il](https://aiisrael.org.il/)
   - [JPost: Project Nexus quantum computer](https://www.jpost.com/business-and-innovation/tech-and-start-ups/article-904774)

3. **The public-sector AI rulebook was published four months ago.** On 19 May 2026 the
   National Digital Agency, the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Innovation published
   the *Guide for Responsible AI Use in the Public Sector v1.0*. It uses a traffic-light risk
   approval system and a supervised "blue track" for experiments. **That guide is the hook
   for the Constitutional Firewall.** The firewall's four gates (credential → sponsor → scope →
   constitution) can be positioned as the enforcement layer the guide describes but does not
   provide.
   - [Lexology: Quarterly AI Update Q2 2026](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=8dc8b6bc-2f7b-48e4-9515-acaf64ba4cb7)
   - [OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026: Israel](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/digital-government-outlook-2026_d46c0555-en/israel_6ab992f0-en.html) ([PDF](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2026/06/digital-government-outlook-2026-country-notes_296a844f/israel_e061484f/6ab992f0-en.pdf)). Israel scores 0.67 on the Digital Government Index, below the OECD average of 0.70.
   - [About the Israel National Digital Agency](https://www.gov.il/en/pages/about-national-digital-agency) · [13 AI projects in ministries, NIS 40M](https://israel.com/technology/13-new-projects-will-integrate-ai-into-government-ministries/) · [Israel Defense: digital government overhaul](https://www.israeldefense.co.il/en/node/68836)

4. **Election law limits what we can do before 27 October.** The 2017 "V15 Law"
   amendment to the Party Financing Law regulates any "organization active in elections":
   anyone polling, mapping voters, or advertising to sway votes during an election period.
   It brings donor caps, foreign-funding limits and State Comptroller reporting. Since July
   2026, campaign material must also disclose AI generation, and the Central Elections
   Committee is actively ordering deepfakes taken down. **So the strategy must be strictly
   non-partisan.** It should be offered to all parties and addressed to "the incoming
   government", and our tech-worker rally must not look like a get-out-the-vote campaign.
   This needs a legal check before any public launch.
   - [Times of Israel: law limiting get-out-the-vote groups](https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-passes-law-limiting-activities-funding-for-get-out-the-vote-groups/) · [Ynet: anti-NGO legislation becomes law](https://www.ynetnews.com/articles/0,7340,L-4938405,00.html)
   - [Library of Congress: Party Financing Law applied to campaign groups (PDF)](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/llglrd/2021700376/2021700376.pdf) · [LoC: campaign finance in Israel](https://maint.loc.gov/law/help/campaign-finance-regulation/israel.php)
   - [IDI: Campaign financing in Israel](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/3255) · [Wikipedia: Party funding in Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_funding_in_Israel)
   - [Haaretz: AI disclosure now required on campaign ads](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-politics/2026-07-27/ty-article/.premium/campaign-ads-must-disclose-ai-use-under-new-election-rules/0000019f-a0a9-dcf3-a9df-a7bb647a0000) · [ToI: labelling AI campaign material](https://www.timesofisrael.com/election-officials-push-to-label-ai-generated-political-campaign-materials/) · [Haaretz: bots and AI robocalls in the 2026 campaign](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/security-aviation/2026-08-11/ty-article-magazine/israel-elections-2026-the-dark-tech-running-israels-election-campaigns/0000019f-eb5f-d101-a5bf-eb5f63800000) · [Deepfakes and election law (S. Horowitz)](https://s-horowitz.com/deepfake-and-election-campaign-law/) · [Central Elections Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Elections_Committee)
   - Note: this also makes clause **I.33 (Truthful Media & Pro-Humanity Content)** directly relevant to Israel right now. That is a strong opening example.

5. **The Start-up Nation story is under strain, which is our opening for a new one.**
   For the first time in a decade, Israel lost high-tech and R&D workers (−5,000 overall,
   −3,500 R&D in 2024). Israeli tech companies now employ more people abroad (~440k) than in
   Israel (~400k). About 35% of companies report more employees asking to relocate. Output and
   exits still hit records. The strategy can offer tech workers a reason to *build here*: a
   national civic mission, open-source, attributed on a public ledger.
   - [Innovation Authority: High-tech report 2026](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/press_release/israeli-high-tech-report-2026/) · [2025 Employment Status Report](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/press_release/2025-high-tech-employment-status-report/) ([PDF](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2025/04/Innovation-Authority-High-Tech-Employment-Report-English-Final.pdf))
   - [Ynet: brain drain grows](https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/bkjjywylge) · [ToI: record emigration of highly educated Israelis](https://www.timesofisrael.com/rising-exodus-of-top-taxpayers-to-cost-israel-billions-in-lost-revenue-study/) · [ToI: war spurs brain drain](https://www.timesofisrael.com/war-spurs-brain-drain-outflow-of-israeli-high-tech-employees-report-finds/) · [Ctech: startups grow, not necessarily in Israel](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/sk8qqitegg)

## 2. Research by domain

### A. Compute, the Innovation Authority, and funds
- **National supercomputer (Telem program):** Nebius built it; 1,000 Nvidia B200s, about 16,000 PF; 70% for industry, 30% for academia; access at below-market prices.
  [IIA press release](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/press_release/supercomputer-access-2026/) · [Nebius selected](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/press_release/nebius-to-establish-supercomputer/) · [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-israel-ai-supercomputer/) · [JNS](https://www.jns.org/israel-offers-subsidized-ai-computing-to-boost-tech-research/)
- **IIA instruments we can map to Humanity-AI work:** Tnufa ideation grants (up to NIS 200k, open until 19 Nov 2026), Magnet (academia–industry consortia, since 1994), incubators and the new Venture Creation Incubators Fund, and the high-tech stimulus package.
  [IIA programs](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/programs/) · [Tnufa](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/programs/ideation-tnufa-incentive-program/) · [Stimulus package](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/press_release/stimulus-to-boost-israeli-high-tech/) · [IIA (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Innovation_Authority)
- **Cyber:** the National Cyber Directorate is the natural co-owner of a government AI firewall. [INCD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cyber_Security_Authority_(Israel))
- Commentary on sovereign-AI investment: [LDS: $30B sovereignty estimate](https://letsdatascience.com/news/israel-commits-30b-to-national-ai-sovereignty-4215c922) (an analyst estimate, **not** an appropriation) · [AI Insider: sovereign AI initiative](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/08/05/how-israel-is-building-a-sovereign-ai-initiative/) · [JPost: 4M workers face job change](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-899354)

### B. Bureaucracy and regulation (the "broken bureaucracy" case)
- Government Resolution 2118 (2014) introduced regulatory impact assessment. The Principles of Regulation Law 2021 created the Israeli Regulatory Authority (ILRA).
  [OECD Regulatory Policy Outlook 2025: Israel](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2025/04/oecd-regulatory-policy-outlook-2025_a754bf4c/full-report/israel_db4adb87.html) · [OECD 2026: drivers of regulatory burden](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2026/06/smart-regulations-strong-business_435e39ce/full-report/diagnostic-drivers-of-regulatory-burden-and-complexity_7296019b.html) · [IDI: Untangling bureaucracy](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/38094) · [IDI: Principles of Regulation Law explained](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/36539) · [Ctech: red tape costs ~$4B/yr](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3762263,00.html) · [Public administration in Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_administration_in_Israel)
- **AI law:** Israel regulates AI sector by sector (the Dec 2023 AI Policy from MoJ and the Innovation Ministry). There is no horizontal AI act. Privacy Protection Law Amendment 13 has been in force since 14 Aug 2025 and explicitly covers AI data use.
  [Nemko: AI regulation Israel](https://digital.nemko.com/regulations/ai-regulation-israel) · [IAPP: Amendment 13](https://iapp.org/news/a/israel-marks-a-new-era-in-privacy-law-amendment-13-ushers-in-sweeping-reform) · [Lexology: 2025 privacy & AI review](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=7cf82836-1f4b-4b84-ac23-4a086a08f226) · [Chambers: Data Protection 2026 Israel](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/israel)
- **Open-source precedent inside government:** the 2014 cabinet resolution to publish government code; the CIOIL GitHub org; Israel's OGP commitment IL0035.
  [gov.il: releasing government code](https://www.gov.il/en/departments/publications/reports/github) · [github.com/CIOIL](https://github.com/CIOIL) · [OGP IL0035](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/israel/commitments/IL0035/)

### C. International comparables (what worked, what is theatre)
- **Taiwan vTaiwan / pol.is:** crowdsourced deliberation that shaped about a dozen laws. It is the closest precedent to our survey mechanism. Its weakness was that government was not required to act, which our strategy should fix.
  [Computational Democracy: vTaiwan case](https://compdemocracy.org/case-studies/2014-vtaiwan/) · [Crowdlaw: vTaiwan](https://congress.crowd.law/case-vtaiwan.html) · [Global Government Forum: Audrey Tang](https://www.globalgovernmentforum.com/the-wisdom-of-crowds-an-interview-with-taiwans-unorthodox-digital-minister/)
- **Estonia:** X-Road plus digital ID; AI always "under the name of a human minister". This matches our "agents propose, humans dispose" rule.
- **Albania's "Diella" AI minister (Sept 2025):** use it as a cautionary tale about AI in a ministerial *title* without accountability.
  [TIME](https://time.com/7324934/albania-ai-minister-diella/) · [EUISS](https://www.iss.europa.eu/publications/commentary/artificial-intelligence-real-politics-what-albanias-ai-minister-means-eu) · [Context/TRF](https://www.context.news/ai/albanias-new-ai-bot-minister-hype-or-help)
- **UAE:** the first (human) Minister of State for AI (2017).

### D. *Start-up Nation* (Senor & Singer, 2009)
- The book's thesis: IDF units such as 8200 and Talpiot, flat hierarchy and *chutzpah*, immigration (especially the 1990s Soviet aliyah), and state-seeded venture capital through **Yozma (1993)**.
  [Wikipedia: Start-up Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Start-up_Nation) · [CFR book page](https://www.cfr.org/books/start-nation) · [Dan Senor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Senor) · [Saul Singer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saul_Singer)
- **Critical lens to add** (from my background knowledge; sources still to be pulled): Dan Breznitz, *Innovation in Real Places* (2021); the "dual economy" argument (high-tech productivity vs. the rest of the economy; Bank of Israel and Taub Center work).
- **Our rewrite:** the 2009 story was *state seeds capital → private founders exit*. The 2027 story is *state opens its own operating system → citizens and builders co-own it*. The Yozma of this era is not a VC fund. It is an open-source civic stack with a public ledger of who built what (HRC I.1 attribution).
- **Copyright:** we write an original narrative that *references* the book. We do not reproduce its text.
